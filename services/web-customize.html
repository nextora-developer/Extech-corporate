// KNOWLEDGE BASE

COMMON QUESTIONS

Is this only for SaaS products? +

No. While SaaS is a common use case, our systems are also built for internal tools, operations dashboards, automation platforms, and custom business workflows.

How do third-party integrations work? +

We connect your system to external tools such as payment gateways, CRMs, ERP systems, or messaging platforms using secure, well-structured integrations.

Can the system grow as my business grows? +

Yes. We design systems in modular layers, allowing new features, users, or workflows to be added without rebuilding everything from scratch.

What do you need to get started? +

We start by understanding your business process — who uses the system, what actions they take, and what results you want. From there, we design the technical structure.

Do you work with existing systems or legacy platforms? +

Yes. We often integrate with or refactor existing systems. When needed, we isolate legacy logic, migrate critical paths, and rebuild modules incrementally to avoid disrupting operations.

Who owns the source code after delivery? +

You do. Once the project is completed and settled, the source code, system logic, and deployment assets belong fully to you. No vendor lock-in.
